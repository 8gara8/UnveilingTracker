SAMPLE: A model proposes an antibiotic that clears resistant infections in mice

SAMPLE page, invented for tests and never fetched from a publisher. DOI 10.1016/ut-sample-ledger-0001.

Our model, Halden Design, ranked about 12 million molecules for their chance of killing drug-resistant bacteria. The team made and tested 40 of the top molecules. The best one cleared a drug-resistant skin infection in 8 of 10 mice. A first safety trial in people is registered as NCT09990101.

Antimicrobial resistance makes some infections hard to treat.
